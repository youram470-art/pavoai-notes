Seedance 2.5 in Korean: Text-to-Video Without the Learning Curve

By Xinyue Zhan, Content Lead at Pavo AI

한국어 사용자에게 AI 영상 도구의 가장 큰 장벽은 모델 성능이 아니라 인터페이스 언어입니다. Seedance 2.5는 그 지점을 겨냥합니다. 프롬프트를 한국어로 쓰고, 장면을 짧게 나눠 생성하는 흐름이 자연스럽게 이어집니다.

The underlying idea is familiar: a prompt becomes a short clip, and you refine it scene by scene. What matters for Korean creators is that the examples, labels and guidance are in Korean, so the first ten minutes are spent making video rather than translating menu items. That sounds minor until you are three iterations deep and cannot remember which setting controls motion strength.

The tool is available at seedance25.kr . For anyone building short-form content for Korean audiences, it is a practical way to test the Seedance family of models without fighting an English-only dashboard.
