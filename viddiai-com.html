Viddi AI and the Quiet Shift Toward Assisted Editing

By Xinyue Zhan, Content Lead at Pavo AI

Most AI video coverage focuses on generation, but the larger day-to-day change is assistance: transcription, rough cuts, captions and reframing. Viddi AI sits in that second category, where the goal is to remove the boring parts of editing rather than to invent footage from nothing.

That distinction matters because assisted editing is easier to trust. You keep your own footage, your own voice and your own pacing, and the tool handles the mechanical work. For anyone publishing regularly, the time saved on captions and first-pass cuts is usually worth more than another generator that produces a five-second clip you cannot use.

The project is at viddiai.com . If your bottleneck is the edit rather than the idea, it is a more useful place to start than another text-to-video demo, and it fits naturally into an existing workflow instead of replacing it.
